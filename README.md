A simple Obsidian theme that keeps everything as default, but fixes two issues:
1. Math expressions are rendered using a black color in dark mode, making them unreadable,
2. Math expressions lack extra margin, making them too close to lines and harder to read, or just less pretty.

The first issue has been reported [here](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/mermaid-math-renders-dark-text-in-dark-theme/105188).
